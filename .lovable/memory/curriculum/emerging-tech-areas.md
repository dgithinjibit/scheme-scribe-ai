## AI

- Named **AI** (never AGI).
- Grades 6-12 only. Grade 6 is an introduction: recognising AI around us, simple data,
  safe and honest use. No programming, no model building at Grade 6.
- 2 lessons/week Grade 6, 3 Grades 7-9, 4 Grades 10-12.
- Strands: 1.0 Foundations of Intelligence, 2.0 Data and Representation,
  3.0 AI Techniques and Programming, 4.0 AI System Design and Projects,
  5.0 Ethics, Society and AI Policy.
- Resource line: `KICD AI (Artificial Intelligence) Learning Area Design <Grade>` — no KLB title.

## Blockchain

- Named **Blockchain** (covers crypto/digital assets as one strand, not as the subject name).
- Grades 6-12, 5 lessons/week (55 lessons per term).
- Strands: 1.0 Records, Trust and Value, 2.0 Blockchain Technology and Cryptography,
  3.0 Digital Assets and Digital Finance, 4.0 Smart Contracts and Applications,
  5.0 Ethics, Law and Society.
- Age progression: G6 records/trust/safety only; G7-8 chain mechanics, cryptography, scams;
  G9 wallets, keys, tokens, consumer protection; G10-11 smart contract programming,
  decentralised finance, tokenisation; G12 enterprise, regulation, capstone, careers.
- Trading and speculation are never taught as an activity — only studied as risk,
  consumer protection and law.
- Resource line: `KICD Blockchain Learning Area Design <Grade>`.

Term split for both areas: Term 1 = strands 1-2, Term 2 = strands 3-4, Term 3 = strand 5.
